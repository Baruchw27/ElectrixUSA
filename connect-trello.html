Connect your Trello to Electrix

Checking your link…

Step 1

Press the button. Trello opens. If it asks, sign in with your own Trello account. Then press the green Allow button.

Open Trello and press Allow
Step 2

Trello now shows a long code. Copy the whole code, come back to this page, paste it below and press Connect.

Your Trello stays private: only you see your Trello tasks in Electrix. The code is saved on the Electrix server only — please don't email or text it to anyone. You can disconnect any time in Trello → Settings → Applications → Electrix USA → Revoke.

Open the Electrix app → Tasks → 📋 My Trello. Your cards show up within a few minutes. Ticking a task off in Electrix also ticks it off in Trello.
